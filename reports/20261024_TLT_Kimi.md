BULL CONVICTION: MEDIUM

### Bear Analyst

The bull is cherry-picking a two-week bounce inside a one-year bear market. The rates data are unambiguous: 2Y +120bp, 10Y +99bp, 30Y +75bp over twelve months — the trend that matters is still up. Real yields at 2.64% (+96bp in a year) mean Treasuries compete aggressively with TLT's 5.16% yield while carrying zero duration risk; a +50bp backup in yields costs TLT ≈ 8.3% of price, which wipes out more than 18 months of dividends. BofA still forecasts an October *hike* — that is not a Fed about to cut. Supply is the structural story ("worst bond crisis since Hamilton"), and the Iran/oil episode showed exactly the correlation flip the bull ignores: yields jumping *with* stocks in an inflation shock. The "haven bid" the bull celebrates came from a tech wobble, not recession. Buying TLT at RSI 82.7 after a +4.6% ten-day run is buying the top of a range below a falling SMA200.

BEAR CONVICTION: MEDIUM

### Aggressive Risk Analyst

The bear is fighting the last war. Cycle timing favors the bold: one full year and +120bp of tightening has already been absorbed, the curve has re-steepened to +31bp — historically the phase where long-duration longs begin to pay — and the market is openly betting *against* the BofA hike call, meaning hawkish news is priced and dovish surprises are not. The short-bond squeeze already started ("the crowded bet against bonds felt it"). A 0.23 equity correlation with real-rate risk skewed to the downside of yields argues this is precisely when duration is underpriced. Sizing into strength, not waiting for a dip that may never come, is how the aggressive account wins this setup.

AGGRESSIVE RISK STANCE: BULLISH

Real yields at 2.64% have room to go higher: every +25bp of real-rate repricing costs ~4.15%, and nothing in the prints shows inflation re-acceleration risk receding — the oil/Iran episode demonstrated supply-side inflation can push yields and stocks down together, exactly the correlation flip that turns a "hedge" into a second equity position. Auction supply into a "worst since Hamilton" fiscal backdrop is a slow structural leak. Capital preservation says wait for confirmation the hiking cycle is actually over.

CONSERVATIVE RISK STANCE: BEARISH
